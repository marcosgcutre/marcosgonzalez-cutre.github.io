Una investigación pictórica que opera desde dentro sobre la historia del arte europeo y sudamericano. No la refuta ni la celebra: la habita, la metaboliza y la erosiona. Cada obra retoma una estructura iconográfica consolidada —Fra Angelico, Leonardo, Goya, Gauguin— y la infiltra gestualmente. A la vez, establece conexiones con la historia del arte sudamericano: Ay Apaec, Tarsila do Amaral, Cildo Meireles. A medida que las capas sedimentan, la imagen original cede y emerge otra cosa: el residuo del gesto.

A pictorial investigation that operates on the history of European and South American art from within. It neither refutes nor celebrates: it inhabits, metabolizes and wears away. Each work takes up a consolidated iconographic structure — Fra Angelico, Leonardo, Goya, Gauguin — and infiltrates it gesturally. At the same time, it establishes connections with South American art history: Ay Apaec, Tarsila do Amaral, Cildo Meireles. As layers sediment, the original image yields and something else emerges: the residue of the gesture.
